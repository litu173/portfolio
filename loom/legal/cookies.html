Please note: this document describes how Loom works today and is written in plain language. It is a starting template — have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction before launching Loom publicly.

The short version

Loom uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser's local storage to make the editor fast. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies, so there's nothing to consent to beyond using Loom.

Cookies we set

NamePurposeTypeDuration
loom_sessionKeeps you signed in. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax — not readable by scripts.Strictly necessary30 days, or until you log out

Local storage

KeyPurpose
loom-projects:<account>A fast local copy of your projects (and the only copy in guest mode)
loom-guestYour guest profile, if you use guest mode

Third parties

Loom pages load fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from jsDelivr. These providers receive your IP address when your browser downloads those files, as with any website; they don't set cookies through Loom.

Your control

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. Removing loom_session signs you out; clearing local storage removes guest-mode projects permanently.
