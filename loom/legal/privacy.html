Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Please note: this document describes how Loom works today and is written in plain language. It is a starting template — have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction before launching Loom publicly.

The short version

  • We collect only what Loom needs to work: your account details and the projects you build.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form — only a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash.
  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no analytics, no selling of data. Ever.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time from Account → Privacy & data.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it's kept
Name, email addressTo create and identify your account and let you sign inAccount store on the Loom server
PasswordTo sign you in securelyStored only as a salted, one-way hash
Bio, avatar colour, preferencesOptional profile details and settings you chooseAccount store on the Loom server
Projects, pages, styles, uploaded imagesTo save and publish the sites you buildProject files on the Loom server, plus a copy in your browser for speed
Session identifierTo keep you signed inAn essential cookie in your browser; a hashed copy on the server

In guest mode nothing is sent to a server: your guest profile and projects stay in your browser's local storage until you clear them.

How we use it

Only to provide Loom: signing you in, saving and publishing your projects, showing your profile and honouring your preferences. If you opt in to product emails we may use your email address for that purpose; you can opt out any time in Settings.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process account and project data to perform our contract with you (providing Loom), the session cookie because it is strictly necessary, and optional emails only with your consent.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal data and don't share it with advertisers. Sites you publish are public by design — anything you put on a published page can be seen by anyone who has the link.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, sessions and the projects you own are deleted from the Loom server immediately. Copies cached in your own browser remain until you clear your browser data.

Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a unique salt; sessions use random tokens in an HttpOnly, SameSite cookie; repeated failed sign-ins are rate-limited; cross-site requests are rejected; account files are never served over the web. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data yourself in Account. You may also object to processing, ask us to restrict it, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us for anything you can't do yourself.

Children

Loom isn't intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell signed-in users about significant changes.
